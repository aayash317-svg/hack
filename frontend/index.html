How the System Handles Your Concern

01

Secure Intake

Submit your report, incident details, and optional evidence. Receive your confidential reference and tracking secret immediately.

02

Human Review

Reports are routed to designated authorities. When multiple reports indicate a recurring pattern, they enter a human-review queue.

03

Accountable Escalation

Confirmed patterns follow a transparent escalation path: HOD (Level 1) → Dean (Level 2) → Higher Authority (Level 3), with every step logged.
